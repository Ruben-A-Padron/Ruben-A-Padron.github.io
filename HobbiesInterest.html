Preserving Stiltsville:

I really love history and building. What better a project than Stiltsville? When I learned about Stiltsville I thought it was just a few rotting houses, but after going on site and seeing the extensive history I learned to love it. I based my Eagle Scout project on Stiltsville and rebuilt all the shutters. Now as the Climate Awareness Club President for my school, I will be going back soon to replace the old house number. I have been multiple times and each time I end up having so much fun. Very blessed to have the exciting oppurtunities I have had on the Jimmy Ellenburg House!

Scouting America:

I have been a boy scout since 1st grade. It has molded me into a large part of who I am today. Some of my greatest moments have been from this program and many more to come. A recent one was being able to go to the National Jamboree. For those who don't know, it is the largest scout camp and only occurs every four years. There was 14,000 scouts there and I was fortunate enough to meet the Chief Executive Scout (Roger Krone), National Commisioner (De Vang), and the Lord Baden-Powell (Great Grandson of the Founder). It was very cool to meet all of them and I had a great time there. Some achievements of mine range in Scouting. I have served as the Assistant Patrol Leader, Patrol Leader, Troop Instructor, Chaplain Aide, Senior Patrol Leader, and currently Troop Guide. I was inducted into the Scouting Honors Society (Order of the Arrow) and hav earned both my Ordeal and Brotherhood. Additionally, I have earned awards for Camporees and come from a very competitive Troop 941. We are one of the largest in the Miami area and I joined nearly 6 years ago at the age of 11. Overall, I could not see where I am today without having the Scouting America Program in my life.

∆ Scouting America ∆ Order of the Arrow ∆

Aviation:

I have been in love with anything flying since I was little. Looking up and seeing the birds move through the air has always amazed me and now I get to experience this wonder and joy for myself. I recently starting flying with Aviator's Zone at KTMB Airport and it has been nothing short of amazing. I have so much new knowledge and many new memories and exciting stories. I have a little over 40 hours and will hopefully solo very soon! While it has been anything but easy, and has required a lot of sacrifice I love it like nothing else. Aviation has taught me a lot about who I am under pressure and what I can achieve. I have been pleasantly suprised on multiple occasions and have slowly been mastering the basics of flight.
