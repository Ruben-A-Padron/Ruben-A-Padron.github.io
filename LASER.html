Project Overview

I learned a lot during this process, this was my first time using Fushion, Glowforge, a Laser cutter, and a caliper. For Fushion, I made 2 different iterations of the puzzle piece. The first being the same shape but small gaps led to the puzzle easily coming apart. I used the caliper to measure the difference between the sketch and the physical gap. I determined the tolerance of the laser cutter was 0.04 mm and then I remade it to fit exactly. I made my final draft and then it came out perfectly fitted.
