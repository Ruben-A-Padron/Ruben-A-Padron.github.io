Design and Innovation: Capstone Project

My final project for the design and innovation course is a tool to help detect center lines of runways and notify pilots of the current wind direction to help pilots practice wind correction landings. This tool will also build the necessary knowlegde on how to perform proper use of rudder and ailerons in landing configurations.
